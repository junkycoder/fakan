Immersion.

Immersion is a concept that allows you to create a more immersive experience for your users by changing the color scheme of your website based on the user's preferences.

Try changing your system's color scheme to see the website change its colors accordingly.

For example, if you are using a dark color scheme, the website will change its colors to match the dark color scheme.

This allows you to create a more immersive experience for your users by adapting to their preferences.

Try it out and see how it works!
